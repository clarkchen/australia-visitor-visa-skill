# 英文起草模板

方括号是事实占位符。按实际情况删改；不能直接作为已确认或已签字文件。只保留有证据的段落。

## 共用信件结构

Applicant: [passport name]
Visa application: [verified subclass and stream]

Dear Visa Officer,

I am applying to visit [destination] from [arrival date] to [departure date] for [actual purpose].

[Insert the applicable purpose paragraph below.]

[State the actual departure, arrival and return dates for this applicant; describe joint travel only for the portions actually shared. Describe booking and accommodation status accurately.]

[Insert one consistent funding paragraph stating who covers which expenses and the evidence. Do not keep contradictory self-funded and sponsored wording.]

[Describe the applicant's actual employment or other current circumstances, documented leave if applicable, and evidenced return arrangements. Do not assume everyone is employed.]

[Describe personal travel history accurately, separating visa grants from actual travel. If applicable, disclose the applicant's own refusals and any later visa grants separately.]

My supporting documents include [evidence linked to relevant claims].

Yours faithfully,
[Applicant name]
[Signature and date, when applicable]

## 纯旅游目的（默认）

I plan to visit [destination(s)] for a holiday, including [actual planned tourism activities], from [arrival date] to [departure date].

[For a solo traveller: State that the applicant will travel alone only if confirmed; omit all companion and relationship paragraphs.]

[For a couple or family: Identify actual companions and relationships, distinguish the shared and separate portions of the trip, and describe booking status accurately.]

## 参会目的

I plan to attend [event] in [city] and [verified participation or presentation activity]. My supporting documents include [actual invitation, registration or presentation evidence available].

[If applicable: My spouse will accompany me for tourism. State their actual application or existing visa status only if verified.]

[If relevant and evidenced: Explain any difference between the employer and salary-paying entity, including verified dates and continuity evidence.]

## 陪同旅游目的

[Companion name and actual relationship] will attend [event]. I will accompany them and undertake [actual tourism plans]. [If true: I am not a registered participant.] [Describe independent activities during the companion's conference and the portions of travel shared.]

## 费用承担：按事实选择或组合

Self-funded: I will cover [specific expenses] using [verified personal funds and evidence].

Employer-funded: My employer will cover [specific expenses for named traveller(s)], as confirmed in [document]. [If applicable: I will cover the remaining expenses using [funds].]

Spouse or other support: [Supporter] will cover [specific expenses for this applicant], as confirmed in [funding statement, relationship evidence and evidence of available funds]. [If applicable: I will cover the remaining expenses using [funds].]

使用前按人、费用项目和承担者核对预算，避免把同一笔余额视为双方各自可重复支配的资金。多人同行时，各人段落必须与同一份费用划分对应；单人不增加其他申请人。
